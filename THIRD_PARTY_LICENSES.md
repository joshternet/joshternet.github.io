## Elsewhere network icons

Joshternet Network cards show brand marks only to identify profile links that participating sites published themselves (`rel="me"`). That use does not imply partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement by those brands.

### Simple Icons (SVG paths)

- Package: [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org/)
- License for icon SVG path data: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Pinned generation source: `simple-icons@14`
- Trademark notice: brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners. Simple Icons does not grant trademark rights.

### LinkedIn

- Mark: official LinkedIn “in” logo geometry for link identification
- Trademark: LinkedIn Corporation
- Brand guidelines: https://brand.linkedin.com/
- Simple Icons no longer distributes LinkedIn; this mark is included under LinkedIn’s brand guidelines for referring to LinkedIn when linking to LinkedIn.
