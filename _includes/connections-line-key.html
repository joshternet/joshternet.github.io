What the lines mean Follow a line, pick a website, or have a look at the links people have published. You might discover two sites that know about each other, or two people working on similar things who have never crossed paths.   • Linked to Each circle represents a participating website. The lines show links JoshBot has observed between websites. Select a website or a line to see what connects them. An arrow points from the website containing a link toward the website it references. Sometimes the connection goes both ways, but plenty of links are one-way, just as they are across the rest of the web.
  • Blogroll A blogroll is a list of websites somebody has chosen to link to; it's one of the old web's better ideas, and a lovely way to find people worth reading.
  • Reply, repost, syndication These lines are replies, reposts, and syndicated copies. An arrow points from the website that published them toward the website it references.
  • Shared topic Dashed lines show websites with published work about similar subjects. That doesn't mean the publishers know each other or have linked to one another—it simply gives you another path worth exploring.
